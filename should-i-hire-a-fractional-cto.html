Should you hire a fractional CTO?

A 6-question diagnostic. Returns one of four recommendations: paid hourly advisor, earlier engineering hire, fractional CTO retainer, or full-time CTO. Free, no email required, runs entirely in your browser.

Effective May 2026 · Reviewed quarterly

Question 1 of 6
How many engineers are on the team today, including you?
Counts anyone writing production code at least 20 hours per week.
Question 2 of 6
Do you have a working product with paying users?
A paid pilot or two paying customers count as yes.
Question 3 of 6
In the last 60 days, have you made the same kind of architectural decision badly twice and can you name the cost in engineer-weeks?
Examples: switched queueing systems twice, replatformed without writing down why, hired the wrong senior twice in a row. The cost should be specific.
Question 4 of 6
How many architectural or hiring decisions per week genuinely need a senior brain in the room?
Decisions where getting it wrong costs more than the cost of having a senior present.
Question 5 of 6
When is your next funding milestone?
Series-A pitch, bridge round, or extended seed close.
Question 6 of 6
What is your monthly engineering and senior-leadership budget?
Total for engineering salaries plus any external advisors or fractional roles.
