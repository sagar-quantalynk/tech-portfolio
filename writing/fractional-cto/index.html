A founder's guide to fractional CTO hiring. The role itself

Once the stage filter is clear, the next decision is shape: full-time CTO or fractional? The mistake is treating the choice as a price comparison. It is a stage comparison, with four company states and a different right answer in each. The essay below walks through all four, the failure modes when the wrong shape gets hired, and a working test that takes thirty seconds.

Cost and pricing

Senior fractional CTO in the EU runs €15K to €25K per month. Senior fractional CTO from India runs €8K to €12K per month at the same seniority. The gap is structural, not promotional, but the shape of the diligence shifts when the operator is offshore. The essay below covers the seven objections that come up in every cross-border discovery call (timezone, IP, governing law, continuity, communication, price, the board) and the procedural answers to each.

Comparison shopping

Buyers compare a fractional CTO retainer with a Toptal-style freelancer network because both are sometimes called outsourced engineering leadership. They are not the same product. Toptal sells you specialists you manage yourself; a fractional CTO sells you senior judgment that owns the engagement. Different buyer states fit each one. The comparison essay walks through the ten axes that distinguish them and gives a three-question working test.

How I think about my retainer practice.

I run a fractional CTO retainer through QuantaLynk from €8K per month, six month minimum after a four week pilot. Two retainers held at any time. The retainer covers weekly 1:1, monthly written architecture review, hiring support on senior roles, board prep on the technical narrative, and vendor evaluations. It does not cover line management of engineers, daily standups, or production code authorship; those are different shapes I refer out.
