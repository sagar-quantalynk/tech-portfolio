Sheet 04AboutThe operator

A solo practice, by design.

The studio operates as a senior solo practice with AI tooling deeply integrated and senior contractors brought in on specific scopes. Continuity is built into the engagement model.

Thirteen plus years shipping production engineering. Architecture chops on real time systems, AI feature integration into shipped products, regulated-industry build constraints, and the senior hires that come with growing engineering teams from two to twenty.

The previous shape, an eighteen person team between 2022 and 2024, was deliberately wound down once AI tooling matured to the point where one senior operator and a tight contractor bench could deliver the focused work scale-ups actually need.

Continuity is contractual, not goodwill. Every retainer includes a written handover plan in the SOW. Every build engagement ships with documentation and runbooks the client team owns at handoff. Three to four engagements per quarter is the cap. The studio walks from anything outside that capacity, regardless of price.

The commercial entity is QuantaLynk. Engineering practice, contracts, and founder-level technical decisions sit here. Reach me on LinkedIn or by booking a 30 minute call below.
